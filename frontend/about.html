About MorphSync

The identity layer for creators

Real-time AI identity masking and avatar streaming for creators, streamers, virtual entertainers, and digital communities who value privacy and creative expression.

🔮 What we build

MorphSync is a real-time AI-powered identity masking and virtual persona platform. We enable creators to transform their live camera feed into stylized, expressive characters during streams, virtual meetings, and content sessions — compatible with OBS, Zoom, TikTok Live, and more.

Our engine preserves natural motion and emotion while completely protecting your real identity. No deepfake detection flags — because the output is intentionally synthetic and clearly labeled as creative expression.

🎭 Who it's for

MorphSync is designed for anyone who wants creative freedom without compromising privacy:

• Content creators
• Live streamers
• Virtual entertainers
• Online communities
• Social media creators
• Privacy-conscious users

The platform operates on a session-based model — activate your identity layer when you need it, with no recurring commitments.

✦ Our mission

Our mission is to make AI-powered virtual presence technology accessible, fast, and enjoyable for creators around the world — without compromising identity or safety.

We are committed to responsible AI usage, platform safety, user privacy, and compliance with applicable laws and platform policies. Every transformation is ephemeral — we don't store your face data.

Built by creators, for creators

MorphSync started as a tool for a small streaming collective who wanted to perform without exposing their personal lives. Today, it serves thousands of creators who value digital autonomy, creative freedom, and identity fluidity.
